The Real Problem

You probably do not need more motivation. You need a system that fits your current life.

Motivation disappears when the plan is too complicated, the schedule breaks, the workouts are too aggressive, or nobody helps you adjust. Body Rebuild starts with your current ability, your equipment, your schedule, and the level of support you actually need.

The Body Rebuild System

Three required workouts. Realistic nutrition. Sixteen weeks of structured progress.

Body Rebuild combines progressive strength training, practical nutrition, and clear progress tracking. You receive a Foundation, Build, or Performance track for Home or Gym, plus one optional Rebuild Accelerator each week.

Training

Three required strength sessions each week, selected for your level and environment.

Nutrition

A realistic structure built around protein, portions, habits, and the tracking method appropriate for your tier.

Progress

Workouts, strength markers, nutrition habits, measurements, and check-ins are tracked so decisions are based on evidence.

The 16-Week Path

Four phases. One direction.

Weeks 1–16, structured from day one.

1–4

Rebuild

Restore consistency, movement confidence, and a schedule you can repeat.

5–8

Build

Add volume, improve nutrition structure, and create measurable momentum.

9–12

Strengthen

Progress the major movement patterns and build higher work capacity.

13–16

Consolidate

Finish strong, document progress, and choose the correct next phase.
