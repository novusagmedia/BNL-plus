The Review Process

What happens after submission

  1. 1

    BNL Plus reviews your goals, schedule, training history, nutrition readiness, support needs, health readiness, and investment timing.

  2. 2

    You receive one of seven outcomes: Approved Essentials, Approved Coaching, Approved Private, Clarification Required, Medical Clearance Required, Not Currently Ready, or Not a Fit.

  3. 3

    Approved Essentials applicants receive the direct enrollment route.

  4. 4

    Approved Coaching and Private applicants receive a 30-minute Fit Call invitation.

  5. 5

    Enrollment begins only after approval, agreement, required screening, and successful payment.

This application is not a medical evaluation. BNL Plus may require written medical clearance when pain, injury, surgery, symptoms, pregnancy-related restrictions, provider restrictions, or other health concerns affect safe participation.

Completing the application does not guarantee acceptance or reserve capacity.
