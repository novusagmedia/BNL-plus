Body Rebuild

A 16-week system for getting strong, leaner, and consistent again.

Body Rebuild is designed for busy adults 35+ who have been inactive or inconsistent and need a clear way back into structured training.

The Promise

The goal is not to destroy you for 16 weeks. The goal is to rebuild a process you can keep.

You will train three required days each week, use a Home or Gym program matched to your level, follow a realistic nutrition structure, and track the evidence that shows whether the plan is working.

What Is Included

Everything is structured. Nothing is random.

Three Required Workouts

Strength training built around squat, hinge, push, pull, single-leg, carry, and core patterns.

One Optional Accelerator

An optional fourth session for conditioning, movement, or extra work when recovery and schedule allow.

Home or Gym

Foundation, Build, and Performance tracks are available for both environments.

Realistic Nutrition

Macro, Portion, or Hybrid methods depending on readiness and tier.

Progress Tracking

Workout completion, performance, nutrition habits, measurements, and check-ins.

Structured strength for real life

Home Equipment

What you need to train at home

Adjustable dumbbells, resistance bands, a bench or sturdy chair, and safe floor space. When that equipment is not available, choose the Gym track.

Training Levels

Your level is assigned from evidence—not ego.

Foundation

For people rebuilding consistency, confidence, work capacity, and basic movement skill.

Build

For people with some recent consistency or previous experience who are ready for structured progression.

Performance

For experienced trainees who can use reps in reserve and progressive overload independently and have no unresolved safety issue.

Nutrition

Nutrition that fits real life

Body Rebuild does not use rigid meal plans or medical nutrition therapy. The program uses protein targets, meal structure, portions, food logging when appropriate, grocery and restaurant strategies, and weekly adjustments according to your tier.

Support

Same program length. Different levels of support.

Essentials is self-directed. Coaching adds weekly review, written feedback, group accountability, and targeted adjustments. Private adds weekly 1:1 support, faster weekday communication, deeper customization, and a monthly live session.
